How we build

Priced where you are

Prices show in your local currency across 54 African countries, and you pay through Paystack. No dollar card needed.

In your language

English, French, Arabic and Portuguese, including right-to-left layouts, so a whole team can use the same tools.

Made for real work

Designed for the phone in your hand as much as the computer on your desk, so your team can work from the office, the site or the road.

Built for the long run

We keep improving the products you rely on, guided by what our customers ask for. Your data stays yours, protected by encrypted connections, and when you need help you reach a real person on our support team.
