Chelkron is built for businesses across Africa, so our prices should speak your currency. From today, every price on chelkron.com and in Chelkron Sites, Chelkron Mail and Chelkron Workforce shows in your own money: cedis in Ghana, shillings in Kenya, rand in South Africa, CFA francs across West and Central Africa, and more.

## Where you'll see it

- **chelkron.com:** the [Pricing](/pricing) and [Packages](/packages) pages and every "from" price.
- **Sign-up pages** for all three products.
- **Billing pages** inside the apps: monthly and yearly prices, savings, offers and coupons.
- **Domains:** search results, renewal prices and checkout.

Prices follow today's exchange rate, updated every day.

## How we choose your currency

We use the country you're visiting from. To see another country's prices, use the **Prices in** picker at the bottom of any page on chelkron.com, or the country picker on each product's own pages.

Inside Chelkron Workforce, prices follow the currency your company uses (**Management → Company profile**). In Chelkron Sites, they follow your website's country.

## Paying

Nothing changes in how you pay. You can still pay by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack or Flutterwave, monthly or yearly.
