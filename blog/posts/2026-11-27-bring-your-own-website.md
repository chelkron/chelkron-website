Not every business starts from a template. Maybe a designer built your website, or you made it yourself. You can now host that website on Chelkron Sites, with your own domain, fast hosting and free security certificates.

## Upload a .zip

Open **Grow → Upload my own website** and upload your website as a .zip file, with **index.html** at the top. We check every file, keep the website files (pages, styles, scripts, pictures, fonts, video and PDFs) and leave out anything that can't run here, then tell you what we did.

Pretty addresses work as you'd expect: **yourshop.com/about** opens **about.html**.

## Edit files in your browser

Need to change a phone number or fix a typo? Open **Website files**. Folders show as a tree; click any page or style sheet to **edit** it right there, or **replace** it with a new copy. Changes go live when you publish.

## Deploy from GitHub

If your website lives on GitHub, connect it under **Deploy from GitHub**. Choose the repository, branch and folder, such as **public** or **dist**. Every push updates your website, either straight away or when you press **Publish**. If a push has a problem, your website stays as it was and we email you why.

## Contact forms just work

Forms on your uploaded website, like a contact or quote form, now arrive in your Chelkron Sites **Messages**, with every field the visitor filled in. You get a notification, and visitors see a thank-you page. Forms that send with JavaScript get the reply they expect, too.

## Who can use it

Uploading your own website is on the **Business** and **Store** plans, once your own domain is connected and your email is confirmed.
