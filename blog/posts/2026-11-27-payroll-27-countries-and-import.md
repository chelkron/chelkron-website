Two updates that make Chelkron Workforce easier to start with, wherever your business is.

## Built-in payroll rules for 27 countries

Workforce now works out income tax and the usual contributions from pay for **27 African countries**, including Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Zambia, Ethiopia, Egypt, Morocco, Cameroon, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal and Zimbabwe.

Each country uses its current tax bands and contributions, such as pension and social security, with the caps and rules that apply there. Where a country reduces tax for family members, as in Côte d'Ivoire and Senegal, the salary form asks for it.

To use it, open **Payroll → Tax and pension** and choose your country. You can still adjust any figure if your company's case is different. As always, check with your accountant for anything unusual.

## Import staff and leads from a spreadsheet

Moving to Workforce no longer means typing everyone in by hand.

1. Open **Management → Import** for staff, or **CRM → Import** for leads.
2. Choose a CSV or Excel file (up to 1,000 rows), or download our template first.
3. Check the preview. We match your columns automatically, even in French or Portuguese, and mark any row that needs attention, saying why.
4. Press **Import**.

New staff can receive their sign-in details by email, in their own language. Staff managers can import too, but only admins can import salaries.
