Tasks are where work gets handed out and followed up in Chelkron Workforce. Two improvements make them more useful: tasks can now carry files, and you can reply to a specific note.

## Attach files when you give out a task

When you add a task, the **Instructions** box now has an **Attach files** button. Add the brief, the price list, the site photos or the template the person needs, so everything is in one place from the start.

- Up to **5 files**, up to **10 MB each**.
- Documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDFs and images are all welcome.
- The files appear under **Instructions** when the task is opened.

## Add files to notes as the work goes on

The notes on a task work the same way. Tap the **paperclip** next to the note box to attach files: a draft for checking, a receipt, a photo of the finished job. You can send files on their own or with a written note.

## Reply to a specific note

As a conversation on a task grows, it can be hard to tell which note someone is answering. Now every note has a **Reply** button.

- Your reply shows a **quote** of the note you're answering, so everyone can see what it refers to.
- Tap the quote to jump back to the original note.
- The person who wrote that note is **notified** of your reply, even if they're not the one doing the task or the one who assigned it.

## Files stay private

Files on a task can only be opened by the people on that task: the person doing it, whoever assigned it, and your company's administrators. They can't be opened by anyone else in your company, or by anyone outside it, even with the link.

## Also new: alerts you'll hear

Notifications on your phone from Chelkron Workforce and Chelkron Mail now play your phone's alert sound and vibrate, so a new task or an urgent email doesn't go unnoticed. See [how to make sure you never miss a notification](/blog/never-miss-a-notification).
