Many of our customers use more than one Chelkron product: a website on Chelkron Sites, email on Chelkron Mail, and Chelkron Workforce for their staff. Until now, that meant three plans, three payments and three renewal dates.

From today, you can get all three together in a **Chelkron pack**, for less than buying them separately.

## Three packs

- **Starter pack:** for a small business getting started. A Sites Starter website with a shop, Mail Starter with 5 mailboxes on your domain, and Workforce Micro for up to 5 staff.
- **Business pack:** for a growing team that sells online. Sites Business (200 products and Paystack checkout), Mail Business with 20 mailboxes, and Workforce Starter for up to 10 staff.
- **Pro pack:** for a busy company with a big team. Sites Store (unlimited products), Mail Enterprise with 50 mailboxes, and Workforce Growth for up to 30 staff.

See the prices in your own currency, and how much each pack saves you, on the [Packages page](/packages). Pay for a year and you get 2 months free.

## How it works

1. Choose a pack on the [Packages page](/packages). Create your Chelkron Sites account, or sign in, and pick the website the pack is for.
2. Pay once, by card, bank transfer or USSD.
3. Open your website's **Plan and payment** page. **Manage email** opens Chelkron Mail, and **Set up Workforce** creates your company's workspace in one click, already on your pack.

Your website, email and Workforce all run until the same date, and renew together.

## Already a Chelkron customer?

Use the same email address for your Chelkron Sites account. Your Chelkron Mail account joins the pack automatically. When you choose **Set up Workforce**, enter your existing workspace's admin password and it moves onto the pack. If you already pay for a bigger Mail or Workforce plan, you keep it.

## If you don't renew

Your website goes offline and your email and Workforce pause. Nothing is deleted. Renew the pack and everything comes straight back.
