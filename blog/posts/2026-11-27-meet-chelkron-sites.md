Most small businesses in Africa sell on WhatsApp and Instagram, but customers still ask, "Do you have a website?" Chelkron Sites is our answer: a real website for your business, ready in minutes, and easy enough to run from your phone.

You can try it free for 14 days at [sites.chelkron.com](https://sites.chelkron.com). No card needed.

## Start from a template, then edit right on the page

Choose what kind of business you run: a shop, a restaurant, a salon, a church, a school, a law firm and more. Sites sets up the pages, the look and the sections for you. Tell us a little about your business and we'll write the first draft of your text too.

To change anything, open **Edit website**. You see your real website and type straight onto it. Click a heading to change it, move a section up or down, or swap a photo. There are no forms to hunt through.

Every template now has richer designs to choose from: photo collage banners, a band of moving words, and **Shop by category** tiles that open your shop on the right category.

## Sell online, the way your customers pay

- **A cart and checkout** that works on any phone.
- **Pay on delivery, bank transfer or card.** Card payments go through Paystack, straight into your own account. Chelkron never touches your money.
- **Orders on WhatsApp** for customers who prefer to chat.
- **Product options** such as size, colour or pack size, each with its own price and stock.
- **Delivery areas** with their own fees and times, free delivery above an amount, and pickup if you prefer.
- **"Your orders"** for buyers, so they can check on an order without calling you.

## Bookings for businesses that sell time

Salons, clinics, consultants and tutors can take appointments online. Set your services, how long each takes, your opening hours and your staff. Customers pick a time that's free, and you can ask for a deposit.

## Reviews that help you sell

After an order is delivered, your customer is asked to rate it. You approve each review before it appears. The stars show on your product pages and can show in Google results too.

## Your own domain, with email included

Search for a name like **yourshop.com** or **yourshop.com.ng** and buy it right inside Sites. We connect it to your website for you. Every Sites plan also includes professional email addresses on your domain, through Chelkron Mail.

## Bring your team

Invite the people who work with you under **Settings → Team**. **Staff** can handle orders, products and messages. **Managers** can do almost everything. Nobody needs your password.

## Made for Africa

Sites works in English, French, Portuguese and Arabic, with every African currency for your products.
