Your team talks all day, so Messages in Chelkron Workforce should be as good as the chat apps they already use. This update brings the features you've asked for, while keeping your company's conversations inside your company.

## See who's around

- A **green dot** shows who has Workforce open right now; others show when they were last seen.
- Tap a chat's name (or **ⓘ**) to open its **info panel**: members, shared **photos**, **files**, **links** and **pinned messages**.
- Groups can have a **photo and a description**. The person who made a group, or an admin, can add and remove people.

## Keep your list tidy

- **Pin** important chats to the top.
- **Mute** a busy chat for 8 hours, a week or until you turn it back on. You'll still be alerted when someone @mentions you.
- Filter to **unread** chats, mark a chat as unread to come back to it, and see a **new messages** line where you left off.

## Write better messages

- **Bold**, *italic*, strikethrough, code and lists, from the **T** button or keyboard shortcuts.
- Send up to **10 files at once**, drag and drop them onto the chat, or paste a screenshot.
- Photos open in a full-screen **viewer**; swipe or use the arrow keys to see the rest.
- Links show a **preview** with the page's title and picture.

## New ways to work together

- **Polls:** ask the team a question and see the votes come in.
- **Scheduled messages:** write now, send at the time you choose.
- **Forward** a message to another chat, and **save** messages to find them again later.
- **Announcement channels:** only admins post, everyone reads. Good for company news.

Open **Messages** in Chelkron Workforce to try it all. Nothing to switch on.
