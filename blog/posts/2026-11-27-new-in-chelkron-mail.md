A round-up of what's new in Chelkron Mail and in Mailbox, our webmail app.

## One signature for the whole company

Account owners can now set a **company signature** under **Mailboxes → Company signature**. Use placeholders like **{name}**, **{title}**, **{phone}** and **{website}**, and each person's details are filled in for them. Choose whether it goes **under their own signature** or **instead of it**. A live preview shows exactly how it will look.

Each person adds their job title and phone number once, in **Signature** in Mailbox.

## Group addresses, on every plan

Aliases and group addresses are now included on **every plan**, Starter included. Make **sales@** deliver to three people, or **info@** to the whole team.

## Send as sales@

People in a group address can now **send and reply as it**. In Mailbox, a **From** picker appears when you write. Replies to an email sent to **sales@** go out from **sales@** automatically. It works in Outlook and on phones too.

## Small things that make a big difference

- **Your own signature** in Mailbox, added to new emails and, if you like, to replies.
- **Colleague suggestions** as you type in To or Cc.
- **Restore from Trash** puts an email back in the folder it came from.
- **Not spam** is easier to find in Junk.
- **Manage your domain** from the Mail domains tab: DNS records, nameservers and transfers for domains bought through Chelkron.

Sign in at [mail.chelkron.com](https://mail.chelkron.com) to try them.
