Your business data belongs to you. You can now download it from all three Chelkron products whenever you like, for your records, your accountant or a backup. You get a .zip file of spreadsheets (CSV files) that open in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers.

## Chelkron Sites

Open your website's **Plan and payment** page and choose **Download your data**. You get your orders, customers, products, bookings, reviews, messages and more, with the option to include your photos and uploaded website files.

## Chelkron Mail

Go to **Settings → Download your data**. You get your domains and their setup checks, mailboxes, aliases and group addresses, a log of who sent email and when, and your payments.

Emails themselves stay in your mailboxes. To keep a copy of them, add your mailbox to Thunderbird or Outlook and back it up there.

## Chelkron Workforce

Admins go to **Management → Workspace → Download your company's data**. You get staff, departments, attendance, leave, holidays, tasks, CRM leads and notes, payroll, appraisals, calendar events, reports and weekly goals.

## What's never included

We leave out passwords and other security details, and anything that isn't yours alone to download, such as team members' private messages.

## Kept safe

Only account owners and admins can download. Chelkron Support can't download your data, even when helping you in your account, and every download is recorded.
