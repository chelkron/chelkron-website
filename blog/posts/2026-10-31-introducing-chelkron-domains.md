Your domain is your business's address on the internet. It's the part after the @ in your email, and the address people type to find your website. Until now, you could buy one inside Chelkron Mail. Today it gets a home of its own: **[domains.chelkron.com](https://domains.chelkron.com/)**.

## What's new

**Buy a domain without signing up for anything else.** Search for a name, pick an extension such as .com, .com.ng or .ng, and check out. You create a simple account as you pay, and you can manage your domain straight away.

**Pay in naira.** Prices are shown and charged in naira, through Paystack. You don't need a dollar card.

**Manage it yourself.** Every domain comes with the tools you need to run it:

- **DNS records:** add, change or remove records, for example to connect a website.
- **Nameservers:** point your domain at any website host you like.
- **Transfer lock:** keep it switched on so nobody can move your domain without you, or get a transfer code if you ever want to move it elsewhere.
- **Renewals:** see when your domain expires and renew it in a few clicks. We remind you before it runs out.

## Add email when you're ready

Many businesses buy a domain first and sort out email later. That's fine. When you're ready for addresses like *you@yourbusiness.com*, click **Start free trial** in your account and add your domain to Chelkron Mail. A step-by-step page shows you the records your email needs, and you can add them from your domain's Manage page. You get a free 14-day trial with no card required.

If you already use Chelkron Mail, nothing changes: you can still buy and manage domains from the Domains page in your dashboard.

## Choosing a good domain

A few tips before you search:

1. **Keep it short and easy to say.** If you have to spell it out on the phone, it's probably too long.
2. **Match your business name** as closely as you can, so customers can guess it.
3. **Think about your customers.** A *.com* works everywhere; *.com.ng* or *.ng* tells people you're a Nigerian business.
4. **Avoid hyphens and numbers** unless they're part of your name. They're easy to forget and easy to mistype.
5. **Buy it before someone else does.** Good names go quickly, and a domain costs far less than changing your name later.
