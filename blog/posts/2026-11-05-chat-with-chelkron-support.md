Getting help should be as easy as sending a message. From today, you can chat with the Chelkron Support team directly from your Chelkron Mail dashboard and from Chelkron Workforce. You don't need to find an email address or leave what you're doing.

## Where to find it

There are two ways to start a chat:

- **The chat bubble** in the bottom corner of the screen.
- **Support** in the menu. On a computer it's in the sidebar; on a phone, tap **More**.

Write your question and press **Send**. We usually reply within a few minutes, and our answer appears right in the chat.

## You won't miss our reply

- If the chat is closed, a **red badge** on the bubble and on **Support** in the menu shows you have a new reply.
- If you've left the app, we'll **email you** a copy of our reply with a link straight back to the chat.
- In Chelkron Workforce, our reply also appears in your **notifications**, on the bell and on your phone if you've turned notifications on.

Your conversation stays in one place, so you can scroll back to an earlier answer at any time.

## Who can use it

- **Chelkron Mail:** everyone with a Chelkron Mail account, including customers who only have domains with us.
- **Chelkron Workforce:** your company's **administrators**. They set Workforce up for your team, so they're the best people to talk to us. If you're a team member with a question, ask your administrator first; they can chat with us if they need to.

## What to ask us

Anything that gets in the way of your work. For example:

- Setting up your email on a phone, Outlook or another app
- Connecting your domain, or a DNS record that won't verify
- Adding staff, payroll, attendance rules or permissions in Workforce
- A question about your plan or a payment

The more detail the better: tell us what you were doing, copy any error message word for word, and include the email address or company name involved. Never send a password in the chat. We'll never ask for it.

The chat is live now. Open your [Chelkron Mail](/mail) dashboard or [Chelkron Workforce](/workforce) and say hello.
