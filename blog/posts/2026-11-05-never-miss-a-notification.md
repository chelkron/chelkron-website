A task with a deadline, a leave request waiting for you, an email from a customer: notifications on your phone make sure you see them in time. Chelkron Workforce and Chelkron Mail's web app can both alert you, and alerts now play your phone's sound and vibrate.

## Step 1: Turn notifications on

You need to do this once on each phone or computer you use.

- **Chelkron Workforce:** tap **Turn on notifications** in the menu (on a phone, under **More**), then tap **Allow** when your phone asks.
- **Chelkron Mail (Chelkron Mailbox web app):** sign in to your mailbox, tap **Turn on new-mail alerts**, then tap **Allow**.

On an **iPhone**, add the app to your Home Screen first. Open it in Safari, tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, and turn notifications on from the app on your Home Screen. iPhones only allow alerts from apps added this way.

## Step 2: Check your phone's settings

If notifications arrive late, arrive silently or don't arrive at all, a phone setting is usually the cause.

**On Android**
- **Battery saver or battery optimisation** can stop alerts while the screen is off. In **Settings → Apps → Chrome** (or the browser you use) → **Battery**, choose **Unrestricted**.
- **Notification sound:** in **Settings → Apps → Chrome → Notifications**, make sure the site's notifications are allowed and set to **sound**, not silent.
- **Do Not Disturb** silences everything while it's on.

**On iPhone**
- In **Settings → Notifications**, find the app you added to your Home Screen (**Workforce** or **Mailbox**) and turn on **Allow Notifications**, **Sounds** and **Banners**.
- Check that **Focus** or **Do Not Disturb** isn't on.
- Make sure the silent switch on the side of the phone is off if you want to hear alerts.

## Step 3: Test it

Ask a colleague to send you a test task in Workforce, or send yourself an email. If the alert arrives, you're all set.

## Still not working?

Turn notifications off and on again from the menu, then tap **Allow**. If you've blocked notifications by mistake, open your browser's site settings for the app and change notifications to **Allow**.

If you're still stuck, open **Support** in the menu and chat with us. We'll help you sort it out.
